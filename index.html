Our Chicken

TAP TO EXPAND

›

Most chicken sold in the US is water-chilled — meaning it's submerged in large tanks of chlorinated water during processing. The bird absorbs up to 12% of its weight in that water. You pay for water weight. You consume chlorine residue.

We use Bell & Evans 100% Air-Chilled chicken only. Each bird is individually cooled with purified air over several hours. No chlorine. No added water. Just pure chicken. Europe has used this method for over 40 years — and is why some countries banned US chicken entirely.

  • No chlorine bath
  • No water weight — you pay for actual meat
  • Better flavor and texture
  • No antibiotics ever
🥩

Full Oil Guide
What's in Your Food Matters

Most meal prep businesses never tell you what oil they use. We not only tell you — we let you choose.

🥑
Avocado Oil
WE USE THIS

Smoke point of 500°F+ — the highest of any clean oil, making it perfect for high-heat cooking. Rich in heart-healthy monounsaturated fats (oleic acid). Anti-inflammatory. Neutral flavor. Contains lutein for eye health. Cold-pressed options available.

BEST FOR: High-heat cooking, searing, roasting, grilling
🥥
Coconut Oil
WE USE THIS

Rich in medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs) — fats your body converts directly to energy, bypassing normal fat metabolism. Naturally antimicrobial and antifungal. Stable at medium-high heat. Does not oxidize easily. Supports gut health and brain function.

BEST FOR: Medium-heat cooking, stir fry, baking
🫒
Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
WE USE THIS

Cold-pressed from fresh olives. Loaded with polyphenols and antioxidants. Oleocanthal in EVOO has natural anti-inflammatory properties similar to ibuprofen. One of the most studied foods for heart health and longevity — the backbone of the Mediterranean diet.

BEST FOR: Finishing, low-heat cooking, dressings
⚠️
Canola / Vegetable / Sunflower Oil
WE NEVER USE THESE

These are highly processed seed oils — extracted using chemical solvents at extremely high temperatures that destroy nutrients and create oxidized, inflammatory byproducts. They are extremely high in omega-6 fatty acids. The average American already consumes 20x more omega-6 than omega-3. This ratio imbalance is directly linked to chronic inflammation — which drives heart disease, gut problems, hormonal disruption, and more. They are cheap. That's the only reason most food businesses use them.

USED BY: Most meal prep businesses, fast food, processed food
